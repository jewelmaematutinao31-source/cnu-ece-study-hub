Learning Objectives

  1. Analyze the three core considerations of DAP: Commonality, Individuality, and Context.
  2. Contrast authentic play-based inquiry with inappropriate "push-down" academic drills.
  3. Evaluate continuous, authentic observational assessment practices vs standardized testing in early childhood.
  4. Formulate reciprocal, respectful partnerships with Filipino families and communities.

1. The Three Core Considerations (NAEYC Guidelines)

Developmentally Appropriate Practice requires early childhood teachers to anchor instructional decisions upon three indispensable pillars:

1. Commonality Universal age-related developmental milestones and cognitive progression shared by all human children.
2. Individuality Each child's unique developmental pace, personality, learning style, family structure, and neurodiversity.
3. Sociocultural Context Familial customs, community traditions, and native language (Mother Tongue) that give meaning to children's experiences.

2. Avoiding the "Push-Down" Curriculum

A major hazard in early childhood education is the push-down curriculum—forcing young children to sit silently for hours completing abstract, repetitive paper-and-pencil worksheets. DAP strongly counteracts this by prioritizing purposeful, teacher-guided play, sensory-motor exploration, and collaborative problem-solving.
