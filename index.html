Concrete Classroom Teaching Scenarios (DAP in Action)

Real-world comparisons contextualized for preschool and kindergarten environments in the Philippines.

Case 1: Early Literacy

Alphabet & Phonemic Awareness

❌ Inappropriate ("Push-Down"):

Four-year-olds forced to sit silently for 45 minutes tracing letter "M" across 5 photocopied worksheets with repetitive handwriting drills.

✅ DAP Application:

Children form "M" using sensory clay, trace in sand trays, find objects starting with "M" around the classroom, and sing Cebuano rhymes like "Malipayong Bata".

Case 2: Early Math

Quantities & One-to-One Correspondence

❌ Inappropriate ("Push-Down"):

Memorizing abstract number equations like "2 + 3 = 5" on flashcards before understanding what the number 2 or 3 actually represents.

✅ DAP Application:

Sorting and counting local seashells (kinhason), buttons, or mango seeds in egg cartons to build concrete physical representations of quantity.

Case 3: Social Guidance

Conflict & Emotional Regulation

❌ Inappropriate ("Push-Down"):

Public shaming on a "sad face" classroom chart or punitive 15-minute isolated timeout when a toddler has an emotional dysregulation episode.

✅ DAP Application:

Co-regulation in a peaceful "Calm-down Corner" (Pahuwayan) with breathing cards, tactile puppets, and teaching feeling vocabulary in Mother Tongue.
